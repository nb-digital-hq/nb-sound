# Trademark Policy

The licence of NB Sound grants rights over the code. It does **not** grant rights over names or logos. This policy
explains how the names **"NB"**, **"NB Digital"** and **"NB Sound"**, and the associated logos (the marks), may be
used. The Spanish reference is [docs/legal/trademark.md](docs/legal/trademark.md).

## Why

The code may be freely used, modified and redistributed under its licence. The marks identify the official
distribution, so that nobody can sell a modified, insecure or abandoned version under the same name to a person or an
institution.

## Allowed without permission

* Referring to NB Sound by name in articles, tutorials, comparisons and educational material.
* Stating truthfully that a product is "compatible with NB Sound".
* Using the unmodified logo to link to the official project.
* Redistributing unmodified official binaries, keeping the name.

## Requires written permission

* Using a mark as part of the name of another product or service.
* Distributing a modified version under the name NB Sound or NB Digital.
* Suggesting endorsement, affiliation or official certification.
* Using the marks in a third party's commercial material.

## Forks

A fork must change the product name, the application identifier (`org.nbsound.desktop`) and the icon before it is
distributed. Attribution such as "based on NB Sound" is welcome.

## Editions

NB Digital's applications for people are open source. NB Digital's governance modules and services for institutions
are licensed commercially. A self-managed institutional deployment of the open-source application is not an
institutional edition.

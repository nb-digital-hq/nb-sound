# Code of Conduct

NB Sound is a community project. People take part from very different contexts and levels of experience, including
people contributing to an open-source project for the first time. The project commits to making that participation
possible without hostility.

## Our standards

* Respectful and professional treatment in issues, pull requests, reviews and every project channel.
* Criticism is aimed at the work, never at the person.
* Patience with people who are learning.
* Harassment, threats and derogatory comments about personal characteristics are not tolerated.

These standards apply equally to occasional contributors and to maintainers: status within the project grants no
additional leeway.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (repository, issue tracker, discussions) and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Reports are sent privately by email to **nbdigitalsas@gmail.com** with a subject that starts with
`[NB Sound][Conducta]`. Messages without that prefix are very likely not to be read. Every report is acknowledged,
investigated discreetly, and the identity of the reporter is kept confidential from the reported person unless that
is practically impossible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful. Decisions are proportionate and explained to the person concerned.
